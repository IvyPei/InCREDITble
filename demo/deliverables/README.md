# Demo deliverables

- [Pitch deck — 10 slides, PowerPoint v4](InCreditble-pitch-v4.pptx)
- [Pitch speaker script — about 2:50](PITCH_SCRIPT.md)
- [Product demo — 2:58, 1080p MP4](InCreditble-demo-v1.mp4)
- [Video subtitles — SRT](InCreditble-demo-v1.srt)

The deck introduces customer pain, market size, competitors, InCreditble, the product, founder-reported discovery from 10+ conversations, customer wishes and next steps. Slide 5 uses the actual website opening. Sources and narrative are included in speaker notes. Customer discovery is not a waitlist or revenue claim.

The video is a first-cut montage of actual prototype screenshots with transitions, English synthetic narration and burned-in captions. It is not a continuous screen recording. The video and deck tell complementary stories and have separate scripts.

All market data, accounts, KYC, assistant responses and trades inside the prototype are simulated. The researched market-size and competitor information in the pitch is separately sourced in its notes.

Files are available to people with access to this repository. Repository hosting does not create a public video link or deploy the interactive website.
