# InCreditble — 10-slide pitch

## Slide 1

TIMING: 10 seconds



InCreditble helps first-time investors understand and buy corporate bonds, starting with a small amount. We are designing for people like our parents, who want a clear first step.



SOURCES AND CONTEXT

Product positioning from founder and current prototype. All functionality shown is simulated.

## Slide 2

TIMING: 22 seconds



Across more than ten customer discovery conversations, I heard frustration about finding bonds, not knowing where to begin, and wanting to invest only a small amount. These comments shaped our question: can a beginner understand a bond purchase without someone coaching them?



SOURCES AND CONTEXT

10+ conversations: founder report in this chat, October 10, 2026. Quotes: founder-supplied customer comments, edited for clarity. No participant demographics, prevalence or conversion rates established.

## Slide 3

TIMING: 18 seconds



The U.S. corporate bond market has twelve point one trillion dollars outstanding. That is the underlying asset market, not our revenue opportunity. Our initial customer is a U.S. adult with savings who is new to individual bonds, especially an older investor seeking a simpler experience.



SOURCES AND CONTEXT

SIFMA, US Corporate Bonds Statistics, published October 5, 2026: outstanding $12.1T as of 2Q26; $66.7B average daily trading volume, YTD through September 2026. https://www.sifma.org/research/statistics/us-corporate-bonds-statistics

## Slide 4

TIMING: 20 seconds



Fidelity already supports filtered bond search. Public offers fractional bonds and AI questions, while Webull offers managed bond portfolios. So small tickets and AI alone are not enough. Our hypothesis is that fewer explained choices and visible payment terms help beginners make a decision they understand.



SOURCES AND CONTEXT

Official sources checked October 10, 2026. Fidelity: https://www.fidelity.com/webcontent/ap002390-mlo-content/20.04/help/learn_searching_fixed_income_inventory.shtml

Public: https://public.com/invest/bonds

Webull managed corporate High Income portfolio: https://www.webull.com/help/faq/11139

Comparisons summarize documented workflows, not execution quality or absence of competitor features. Webull also offers separate self-directed bonds. InCreditble $10 minimum is a prototype design, not a live brokerage offering.

## Slide 5

TIMING: 10 seconds



Meet InCreditble: a corporate-bond trading experience for first-time investors. Start small, choose bonds around your goals, and get help in plain English.



Source: actual prototype opening, demo/media/welcome.jpg. Static screenshot. All trading, account connections and assistant responses are simulated.

## Slide 6

TIMING: 20 seconds



Users tell us their experience, yield target, timeframe, industry and budget. The prototype returns up to three matches and explains the differences. Optional assistant responses help users understand a company and what could go wrong. Explanation depth adapts to experience.



SOURCES AND CONTEXT

Current prototype and actual demo screenshot. Matching uses deterministic filters and ranking. Assistant responses are simulated; no live model connected. Three matches are a selection limit, not a claim of market-wide optimality.

## Slide 7

TIMING: 20 seconds



A twenty-five-dollar budget buys part of a bond in the demo. Before approval, the user sees the final cash cost, expected interest and repayment date. After confirmation, cash and holdings update, and the payment schedule includes the new position.



SOURCES AND CONTEXT

Actual prototype transaction captured October 10, 2026: $25 budget, $24.99 cost, $24.96 face, $2,475.01 cash remaining, $0.56 expected semiannual interest. Prices and dates are fictional demo data. Fractional execution and custody in a live product remain unvalidated.
