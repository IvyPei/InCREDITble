# InCreditble customer-led pitch and demo

**Target duration: 2:58.** English narration with a customer-discovery opening and one complete $25 simulated purchase. Timings are a recording plan, not a completed video measurement.

Customer quotations come from the founder’s supplied comments and are edited for clarity on screen. See [quote notes](video/QUOTE_NOTES.md). Four comments are not labeled as four participants.

[Clean voiceover](video/voiceover.txt) · [Draft subtitles](video/captions.srt) · [Recording instructions](video/RECORDING.md)

## 0:00–0:25 Customer voices

**Screen and action:** Show quote cards 01, 02, 03 and 04 in sequence. Keep each on screen for about six seconds. Quotes are edited for clarity; do not invent names, ages or interview counts.

**Say:**

“In customer discovery, I heard: Finding a bond’s CUSIP is such a pain. I don’t know where to buy. Fidelity’s bond account is not for me. I only want to invest a small amount. Different comments, one design challenge: getting started.”

## 0:25–0:37 The design question

**Screen and action:** Show the welcome with Your first bond. At your pace. Enter Overview, point briefly to the sample connected account, then click Find a bond.

**Say:**

“How can someone with no bond experience understand a few options and make their first small purchase? That’s the question behind InCreditble.”

## 0:37–0:59 Start with the person

**Screen and action:** From a reset demo, complete Continue with a demo identity. Keep I’m new to bonds and Balanced. Click Choose my preferences. Keep 4.50%, up to 5 years and Any sector; change amount to $25. Click Find my bonds.

**Say:**

“First, I tell the product I’m new to bonds. That changes how much explanation I get. My risk preference is separate. Then I choose a yield target, a timeframe, an industry and a budget. Let’s start with just twenty-five dollars.”

## 0:59–1:20 Replace the search burden

**Screen and action:** Show Marketlane Stores · 1Y, Cedar Healthcare and Sunfield Foods · 2Y. Scroll slowly enough to read the comparison labels. Return to Marketlane and click Ask AI.

**Say:**

“I don’t need to start with a bond identifier. The finder gives me three matches within my preferences. I can compare an earlier repayment date, a different industry and more interest, with the important trade-offs visible.”

## 1:20–1:39 Explain before the decision

**Screen and action:** Choose What are the risks? Pause on the actual answer. Click Back to bond details. Point briefly to the company description. Click Choose an amount.

**Say:**

“When I need help, I can ask about the company or what could go wrong. Here, the assistant explains the risk of missed payments and selling early. The explanations are optional; the decision stays with me.”

## 1:39–2:07 A small purchase with clear terms

**Screen and action:** Wait for the demo quote. Continue with this price. Keep $25 and click Review simulated order. Pause on Total you pay, Cash afterward and payment preview. Check the simulation acknowledgment and click Confirm simulated buy.

**Say:**

“Now I see a sample price, with separate buy and sell quotes. My twenty-five dollars buys part of a bond. Before confirming, I can see exactly what I pay, the cash I’ll have left, expected interest and the repayment date. These are promised payments, not guaranteed returns.”

## 2:07–2:25 Close the loop

**Screen and action:** Click See updated holdings. Show the new Marketlane position and cash change. Click Payments, select April, and point to the new expected interest entry. Avoid showing a second transaction.

**Say:**

“After I approve, the new holding appears and my cash balance updates. The payment view helps me see when interest is expected. Existing sample accounts stay visible alongside this position, so I can see the wider picture.”

## 2:25–2:46 What works underneath

**Screen and action:** Hold on the product or use a simple caption: Filters → Explained matches → User confirmation → Updated holdings. Keep a clear prototype label; do not show invented architecture or provider logos.

**Say:**

“Underneath, explicit filters and transparent ranking narrow a pool of 672 fictional bonds. The prototype tracks fractional positions, settlement, cost basis and payment schedules. Assistant responses, identity checks, quotes and connections are simulated; no live AI model or broker is connected.”

## 2:46–2:58 Return to the user

**Screen and action:** Show closing card 05. End at 2:58 or earlier; no long outro. Do not add a claim of validated customer adoption.

**Say:**

“Next, we’ll test whether first-time investors can explain their purchase and its risks without coaching. InCreditble. Your first bond. At your pace.”

## Rehearsal priorities

Read naturally, with pauses while the judge reads the price and payment information. If a take runs over three minutes, shorten scrolling and repeated explanations; retain the customer problem, actual order review, explicit confirmation and changed holding. The script and captions do not replace the video recording.
