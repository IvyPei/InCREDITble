# Record the customer-led demo

## Deliverable and timing

Record one English video at 1920 × 1080 where practical, with a target duration of **2:58**. The opening uses customer-discovery comments, followed by one complete $25 simulated purchase. Use `../PITCH.md` for the timed script and `voiceover.txt` for uninterrupted narration.

The supplied `captions.srt` is based on the planned timing. The [playable first cut](../deliverables/InCreditble-demo-v1.mp4) and [export subtitles](../deliverables/InCreditble-demo-v1.srt) are included in the demo bundle: 2:58, 1920 × 1080, actual prototype screenshots, transitions, English synthetic narration and burned-in captions. This is a screenshot montage, not continuous screen recording. Use the instructions below for a future pass with continuous recording and founder narration.

## Prepare the product

1. Start the server using the root README. Use variant A.
2. In Your account → Settings, reset demo progress, then refresh to the welcome. This clears your previous demo activity intentionally.
3. Rehearse once: simulated identity → new to bonds → Balanced → 4.50% / up to 5 years / Any sector / **$25**.
4. Reset again before recording. Keep the browser and any presentation window at readable sizes. Disable notifications and hide unrelated tabs.

## Record in three pieces

**A — Opening, 0:00–0:25.** Use the four numbered PNG cards, about six seconds each. Their SVG originals are editable. Add simple cuts or short fades; let the words carry the story. The attribution label must stay readable. Customer voices are quoted by the presenter, not presented as fabricated customer audio.

**B — Product, 0:25–2:46.** Capture the actual screen actions in the script. A single successful purchase is the spine. You can edit out long scrolls or hesitation, but preserve the visible sequence of preferences, matches, explanation, price review, user confirmation and changed holdings. Keep demo disclosures visible. Do not call a montage of still images a live recording.

**C — Close, 2:46–2:58.** Use card 05 and the last two sentences of the narration. Finish without a long logo animation.

Recording the product silently first, then recording your narration over the edited clip, makes it easier to hit the time limit. Leave at least 2–3 seconds on the final cost, payment information and updated holding. Keep background music low or omit it.

## Exact product actions

- After the welcome, briefly show Overview, then Find a bond.
- Simulate identity, choose I’m new to bonds, keep Balanced, continue to preferences.
- Keep the default yield/horizon/industry. Change Amount to invest to **25** and submit.
- Show the three matches. On Marketlane Stores · 1Y, click Ask AI → What are the risks? → Back to bond details → Choose an amount.
- Wait for the quote → Continue with this price → Review simulated order.
- Show the final amount, cash afterward, interest and principal repayment. Check the simulation acknowledgment → Confirm simulated buy → See updated holdings.
- Select Payments, then April, to show the new expected coupon alongside other sample payments.

Do not memorize a quote or amount of interest. Read the actual values because quote refreshes and prior local trades can change them. If the first matches differ, reset rather than forcing a scripted security through filters.

## Export and submit

Use MP4/H.264 with clear speech, legible type and an actual duration between 2:00 and 3:00. Check the whole export once, including subtitles. Confirm the share link works without an access request. If using an external submission host, add its viewable URL to the root README, then rebuild the package with `python3 scripts/package_demo.py`.

The playable draft is included in this repository and its demo bundle. External submissions may need a separately hosted share link accessible to their reviewers. Replacing the synthetic voice and screenshot montage with founder narration and continuous screen recording remains an optional next production pass.
