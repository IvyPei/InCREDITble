## How the demo answers the comments

- Identifier/search friction → yield, timeframe, industry and budget → three matches.
- No experience or starting point → familiarity setting, concise company context and optional explanations.
- Existing interface does not feel suitable → a focused beginner journey; this is a design response, not evidence of competitive superiority.
- Small amount → a $25 fractional simulated purchase, with the $10 demo minimum visible.

The testable outcome is whether users understand the purchase and its risks. Interviews and a polished interface do not establish that outcome yet.
