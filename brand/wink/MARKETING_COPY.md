# InCreditble / Campaign copy

All copy below is for concept testing. No campaign has been published or sent.

## Core message

**Your portfolio. Your payday.**

Bond investing. Everyday cash flow.

InCreditble is a product concept for exploring Treasury and corporate bond portfolios around your life. Start with an amount, choose your comfort with risk, and see an estimated after-tax interest equivalent in a rhythm you understand.

Primary CTA: **Explore the prototype**. Secondary CTA: **Help shape InCreditble**.

## Landing page

Headline: **Give your savings a schedule.**

Body: Your investment plan should connect to the life you want to live. Explore how a bond portfolio could support your cash-flow goals, see the bonds behind the estimate, and understand the risks before making a decision.

Three benefits: Choose a rhythm. Understand the trade-offs. See where the money comes from.

Disclosure: Concept only. No live investing or payment service. Estimates use synthetic data and simplified taxes. Returns and payments are not guaranteed. Bond values can fall and issuers can fail to pay. A transfer may include principal.

## Interview prompts

1. What do you think the large dollar amount means? Is it guaranteed? Does it include your original investment?
2. Move the risk slider. What changed, and what might you lose?
3. Pick a payment frequency. Do you expect the bonds themselves to pay that frequently?
4. What would you need to verify before considering this with your savings?
5. Which would you prefer: a smaller payment with less uncertainty, or a higher estimate with more chance of loss? Why?
