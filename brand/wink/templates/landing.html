Card and cash-account features are concepts. No banking, card issuance, brokerage, or payments partner is connected.

Clear about the trade-offs.

Is a payout the same as investment income?

No. A transfer can include interest, existing cash, and returned or spent principal. InCreditble separates these in the plan so you can see what you would be spending.

Are payouts or my capital guaranteed?

No. Bond prices can change, issuers can fail to pay, and the cash available may not meet your goal. A plan may require a lower payout, more capital, or planned principal spending.

Can I open an account now?

No. This is an interactive product concept for customer discovery, using synthetic data. You can explore the experience without submitting personal information.
