# InCreditble / WINK

Approved direction 02. Brand kit v1.0, October 2, 2026.

## Identity

A tilted, rounded square with a crescent eye and a small smile: a confident wink that makes bond investing feel approachable. The eyes and smile are real transparent cutouts. Use the supplied geometry; the -15 degree tilt is part of the mark.

Write **InCreditble**, pronounced like “incredible.” Repository spelling remains InCREDITble. The audience is people with savings who want understandable, regular cash flow.

**Your portfolio. Your payday.**

Descriptor: **Bond investing. Everyday cash flow.**

Supporting line: **Give your savings a schedule.**

## Logo use

Use the horizontal Ink logo on light surfaces. Use the white variant on Ink or Violet. Use the symbol for app icons and avatars; the wordmark for narrow headers. Black is available for single-ink reproduction. PNGs have transparent backgrounds; SVG masters have outlined wordmarks.

Allow at least 25% of the symbol's width as clear space. Prefer a symbol of 32 px or larger. Use the specially opened small symbol for 16-24 px favicons; some crescent detail is intentionally lost at 16 px. Horizontal minimum: 160 px wide on screen; 35 mm in print, subject to a physical proof. White marks have an enlarged eye opening for optical balance.

Do not stretch, mirror, add extra rotation, redraw the eye, add gradients or shadows, or place the logo on busy imagery. The smile must remain a cutout, not a painted background color. Do not use the logo as a risk rating or an investment endorsement.

## Typography and composition

The supplied wordmark is outlined from Avenir Next Bold. Templates use Avenir Next with Arial/sans-serif fallbacks. No font software is included. Confirm font licensing for deployment and redistribution; the bundle does not certify font rights. SVG campaign text remains editable; PNG exports preserve the rendering.

Use short, large headlines, generous margins, rounded panels, and one main message per layout. The WINK may be enlarged as a graphic; keep faces away from text. Avoid stock ticker wallpaper, rockets, and upward-only charts.

## Voice and financial meaning

Warm, clear, a little playful. Say what people can explore and show the trade-offs.

| Use | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Explore your estimated cash flow. | Guaranteed passive income. |
| Know what comes from interest and principal. | Your money pays you forever. |
| Choose your comfort with risk. | More risk always means more return. |
| Explore the prototype. | Open your account today. |

“Payday” means a proposed transfer, not wages or a promised return. Transfers can include interest, existing cash, and returned or spent principal. Clearly distinguish them. Weekly or biweekly display does not mean the underlying bonds pay that often.

This is a discovery prototype. No live quote, account, broker, card issuer, payment network, regulatory registration, insurance, customer traction, or return guarantee is claimed. Pair social graphics with the full captions in MARKETING_COPY.md. The 2%-10% portfolio range is invented for interaction testing, not market data or expected total return.

## Delivery

Use SVG for scalable logos, PNG for social sharing, and PDF for concept sheets. Social exports are RGB in their named pixel dimensions; stationery and card artwork are concept layouts, not printer-ready CMYK files with bleeds. The card has no issuer or payment-network affiliation. Email templates need hosted image URLs, approved sender/contact details, and unsubscribe infrastructure before use.

The logo has not undergone trademark clearance. Test recognition and name comprehension with customers before launch. The original exploratory image is recorded in ../concepts/wink-refinement; final masters are in this folder.
