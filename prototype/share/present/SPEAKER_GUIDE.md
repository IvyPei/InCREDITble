## 1. Opening · 0:00–0:05

InCreditble makes a first corporate-bond investment easier to understand.

## 2. Problem · 0:05–0:21

In more than ten customer conversations, people told us they wanted bonds but did not know where to start. Bond codes, unfamiliar platforms and minimum amounts made the first step feel difficult. Here is how we simplify it.

## 3. Product demo · 0:21–1:29

Click the video once. Let the 68-second product journey play. When it finishes, advance to Market. The standalone 2:25 demo remains available on the sharing page.

## 4. Market · 1:29–1:39

The U.S. corporate-bond market is over twelve trillion dollars. Our starting audience is adults new to individual bonds, including older investors.

## 5. Competition · 1:39–1:53

Existing platforms offer inventory, ETFs or managed portfolios. We are testing a different entry point: a small amount, three explained matches, and a decision the user understands.

## 6. How it works · 1:53–2:13

The prototype filters by goals and risk limits, ranks a fictional bond pool, and explains the matches. Fractional orders show cost and expected payments before approval. Today, matching is deterministic and the assistant is scripted. Live data and execution come later.

## 7. Early traction · 2:13–2:28

Our evidence so far is discovery, not revenue: ten-plus conversations and a working prototype. Those conversations shaped the priorities: plain language, small starting amounts, payment visibility, and keeping existing accounts visible.

## 8. Next step · 2:28–2:40

Next, we want ten beginners to test the flow without help. Can they explain the cost, payment timing and loss risk? We are seeking pilot participants and bond-infrastructure introductions.
