Sample search assumptions & issuer exclusions

The slider changes a synthetic risk budget, a duration limit, and a speculative-allocation cap. A grid search chooses the highest estimated net-interest portfolio that fits them. Risk scores and 2–13% asset coupons are invented to demonstrate behavior; resulting portfolio gross yield is capped at 10%. These are not market yields, credit ratings, or expected total returns. Not every yield is reachable at every risk setting.

A three-month interest-equivalent reserve is prefunded from your principal, with a 0.10% initial cost assumption. Frequency changes only the annualized display; 52 weekly and 26 fortnightly periods are used. Actual years can contain an extra payment and require a dated cash ledger. This explorer does not replace or synchronize with the separate five-year payday planner.

ABOUT THIS CONCEPT
A plan to explore, not a promise.
This is a throwaway product prototype. All yields, issuers, allocations, and results are synthetic. It assumes fixed illustrative rates, no defaults, at-par bonds, and a 0.10% initial cost. Unpaid net income accumulates as cash; reinvest mode compounds annually. The five-year model rolls maturities at unchanged rates; the first-year ledger shows cash timing. Taxes are simplified provisions, not actual withholding. No market pricing, credit analysis, suitability assessment, or trading is connected.
