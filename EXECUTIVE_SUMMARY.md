# InCREDITble — Product Executive Summary

**Your portfolio. Your payday.**

**Version:** 0.1 — interactive concept prototype

**Date:** October 2, 2026

**Initial market:** United States retail investors

**Stage:** Customer discovery; no live investment or payment service

## The product

InCREDITble is a proposed cash-flow planning and investing product that connects a personalized bond portfolio to everyday spending. Customers choose the money they want to receive, when they want it, and how much capital they want to preserve. The product proposes a portfolio of U.S. Treasuries and corporate bonds, projects its cash flows, and schedules planned weekly or monthly transfers into a separate cash account linked to a debit card.

The customer experience begins with a future spending need rather than a CUSIP or a list of securities. Customers can inspect the bonds, express issuer preferences, exclude a security, or explore another proposed portfolio without becoming their own bond portfolio manager.

## The customer problem

The initial customer hypothesis is an individual with investable savings who wants regular, spendable cash flow and less exposure to equity-market swings. Their investable amount, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, and willingness to pay still need validation.

The founder has conducted three informal customer interviews. Reported difficulties included tracking CUSIPs, understanding whether a bond price was fair, and finding the bond-buying experience within a brokerage. At least one interviewee had purchased an individual bond. The interviews do not yet establish the frequency or severity of each problem, demand for a new account, or willingness to switch providers.

The founder's additional hypothesis is that scheduled deposits into a spending account make investment proceeds more tangible and usable than an account balance customers are reluctant to draw down. This behavior has not yet been validated.

## How it works

1. **Design a payday.** Set available capital, payout amount, weekly or monthly frequency, start date, and desired capital remaining. Choose cash payouts or reinvestment.
2. **Understand the plan.** Review estimated after-tax income, scheduled transfers, principal repayments, remaining capital, funding gaps, and risks.
3. **Personalize optionally.** Request another plan, prefer an issuer, or exclude a bond or issuer. Alternatives retain the customer's goals and constraints, or explain why those cannot be met.
4. **Keep investments visible.** A Portfolio account shows bond holdings, coupons, maturity dates, and the payout reserve. Exploring a proposal does not change held assets.
5. **Use the money.** Planned transfers arrive in a Cash account linked to a debit card for everyday purchases. The spending balance is distinct from invested assets and the portfolio's reserve.

## Why the founder

The founder brings institutional corporate-bond portfolio-management experience, including portfolio construction and relative-value swaps. The intended advantage is translating that expertise into understandable cash-flow plans and disciplined portfolio maintenance. Whether institutional methods produce sufficient customer benefit after retail execution costs, taxes, and portfolio-size constraints remains to be demonstrated.

Treasuries provide both an investable option and a comparison baseline. A corporate allocation should justify its additional risks and costs; the product should permit a Treasury-only plan to be the better fit.

## What version 0.1 demonstrates

The local prototype includes an interactive payday planner; three alternative interface layouts; synthetic Treasury/corporate plan comparisons; issuer preferences and exclusions; estimated cash-flow and capital projections; funding-gap messages; and payout/reinvestment controls.

It also includes a separate illustrative Portfolio account and a Cash account with a debit-card mockup, activity history, freeze/unfreeze controls, and a simulated card purchase. Simulated purchases reduce cash without selling bonds. Proposed portfolio changes leave sample holdings unchanged.

All financial inputs and holdings are illustrative. There is no live optimizer, brokerage connection, bank account, card issuer, trading, tax service, persistent account storage, or real payment functionality.

## Business hypothesis and next milestone

Potential revenue models include a transparent portfolio-management fee, a subscription, or a combination; pricing is undecided. Card-related revenue, if available through future partners, is a possible secondary source and is not established. No revenue, assets-under-management, or customer-adoption claims are made.

The next milestone is to test whether customers can distinguish income from principal spending, understand the reserve and risks, adjust an infeasible plan, and see sufficient value to fund an account. In parallel, the team must validate actual portfolio economics and define the investment, brokerage, custody, banking, and debit-card operating model.

**Product principle:** planned cash transfers are not guaranteed income. Interest earned, principal returned or spent, and cash available to spend must remain clearly separated.
